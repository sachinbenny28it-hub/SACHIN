SACHIN remembers what you choose to share.

Not "SACHIN knows all your secrets." Every memory is something you told it, on purpose, and you can see, edit, or delete any of it at any time — or turn memory off entirely for a conversation.

  • Microphone capture starts only after you press Talk or turn on voice activation. You can turn voice activation off anytime.
  • Browser speech recognition may use your browser's speech provider. When that is unavailable, a short recording is converted to WAV in memory and transcribed by SACHIN's local Ollama service; it is not sent to a cloud AI provider.
  • Your memories and conversations are yours alone — no other account can see them.
